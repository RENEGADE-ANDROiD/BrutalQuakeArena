Brutal Quake overlay for Arcane Dimensions
==========================================

Copy these files ON TOP of rerelease/ad/ (AD v1.80).
Do not put them in id1. Do not put game ad in Steam Launch Options.
Do not replace pak0.pak (AD maps, models, and original QC stay in the pak).

1. Install Arcane Dimensions as rerelease/ad
2. Copy this folder's contents into rerelease/ad
3. Launch the remaster, open the console, type: game ad
4. Start a new game or: map start
5. Turn Enhanced Models off (Options > Display)

Brutal melee (impulse 50), meathook (impulse 52), and gore work.
AD monsters and special guns are remapped to vanilla/Brutal equivalents
(grunts, enforcers, shamblers, lightning, etc.). Mapped nailgrunts and
defenders keep that role if the Q2 mix replaces them. Breakable spawners
are solid brushes (they break and fire targets). This is Brutal combat
on AD geometry, not AD weapons or puzzle QC.

The remaster is not QSS. Large maps (ad_tears, hub) can hitch or crash.
The QSS CSQC HUD will not run. Jump if the hub looks stuck.

This overlay does not include the AD maps.
