Brutal Quake overlay for Liminal Spaces Jam (Alkaline)
======================================================

Copy these files ON TOP of rerelease/limjam/ (the jam pack).
Do not put them in id1. Do not put game limjam in Steam Launch Options.
Do not replace pak0.pak or pak1.pak (Alkaline content and VHS HUD).

1. Install the jam as rerelease/limjam
2. Copy this folder's contents into rerelease/limjam
3. Launch the remaster, open the console, type: game limjam
4. Start a new game or: map start
5. Turn Enhanced Models off (Options > Display)

Brutal melee (impulse 50), meathook (impulse 52), and gore work.
Alkaline monsters and guns are remapped to vanilla/Brutal equivalents
(nailgrunts, ogres, shamblers, etc.). Instant teleporters and
breakables still function.

Puzzles, ladders, fog, rotating machinery, mapvars, and the VHS CSQC
HUD will not behave. The pack was not designed for coop. Large maps
(especially lim_daviddg) may hitch on the remaster.

This overlay does not include the jam maps.
